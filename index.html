🌿 Things I Do for Dhara

Small actions, big change! Here's how I care for our Earth every day.

🌱

Planting Trees

I plant saplings in our garden and neighbourhood parks to give back green to our Earth.

💧

Saving Water

I turn off taps when not in use and remind my family to not waste even a single drop.

♻️

Waste Segregation

I participate in waste segregation drives and make sure wet, dry and hazardous waste is always separated.

🚫🛍️

Saying No to Plastic

I always carry a cloth bag to the market and refuse single-use plastic bags and straws.

🌞

Saving Electricity

Lights off when not needed, fans off before leaving — every unit of power saved matters!

🐝

Helping Wildlife

I put out water bowls for birds and insects during summer and grow pollinator-friendly flowers.

🤝 Adya's Earth Pledge

  • ♻️ I will reduce, reuse, and recycle every day.
  • 💧 I will never waste water or electricity.
  • 📢 I will spread awareness among my friends and family.
  • 🌱 I will plant at least one tree every year.
  • 🤗 I will inspire others to care for our planet.

💡 Quick Eco Tips

Simple things YOU can do starting today!

🚿

5-Minute Showers

Shorter showers save thousands of litres every year. Set a timer!

📚

Digital over Paper

Use digital notebooks and e-books when possible to save trees.

🥗

Eat More Plants

Even one meatless meal a week reduces your carbon footprint!

🌿

Compost Kitchen Scraps

Turn vegetable peels and food waste into rich compost for plants.
