Hi! I'm Adya — a 12-year-old from Bangalore who believes that every small action counts when it comes to protecting our Earth. 🌍

I created DharaCare as a little corner of the internet where I can share my journey — the things I do, what I learn, and why I care. Dhara (धरा) means Earth in Sanskrit, and this website is my love letter to her.

I actively participate in waste segregation drives in my neighbourhood — because I believe the first step to a cleaner planet starts right at home, with the right bin. I also love teaching younger kids not just maths, but also why it's so important to sort your waste and reduce plastic. Watching their eyes light up when they understand something — whether it's fractions or composting — is the best feeling!

I'm just a regular 7th grader doing my bit — one sapling, one segregated bin, one curious kid at a time. 💚
